# Stakeholder Map

Ratings are my judgement for this project, not survey results.

| Stakeholder | Interest | Influence | What they need | How we engage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Health board operations managers (primary user) | High | High | Site-level monthly view, comparison with other boards, early warning | Dashboard + one-page summary |
| ED clinical leads | High | Medium | Evidence to support staffing and patient-flow changes | Volume vs wait analysis, seasonal view |
| Health board executives | Medium | High | Board ranking and direction of travel | One-page memo |
| Scottish Government (health department) | Medium | High | National picture against the 4-hour standard | Context only, not a direct user |
| Patients and public | High | Low | Shorter waits | Out of scope for this tool |
| Public Health Scotland (data owner) | Low | Medium | Their statistics used correctly | Follow PHS data notes and definitions |

## Power / interest grid
- **Manage closely:** operations managers, executives
- **Keep satisfied:** Scottish Government
- **Keep informed:** clinical leads, patients
- **Monitor:** PHS

## Main tension
Executives want a single ranking; clinical leads know that sites differ (for example a children's hospital is not comparable with a general ED). The dashboard needs a ranking view and a clear caveat on comparability.
